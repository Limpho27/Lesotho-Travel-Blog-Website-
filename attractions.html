Top Attractions

Discover the beauty of Lesotho through breathtaking waterfalls, towering mountains, and unforgettable cultural adventures across the Kingdom in the Sky.

Maletsunyane Falls

Located near Semonkong, Maletsunyane Falls is one of Africa’s highest single-drop waterfalls. Visitors enjoy hiking, photography, sightseeing, and the famous 204-meter abseiling adventure.

Learn More

Maluti Mountains

The Maluti Mountains offer breathtaking scenery, hiking trails, fresh mountain air, and snowy landscapes during winter, making them ideal for nature lovers.

Learn More

Pony Trekking

Experience traditional Basotho culture by riding mountain ponies through remote villages, valleys, rivers, and scenic highland landscapes.
