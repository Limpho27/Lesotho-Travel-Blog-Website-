Category Information
Accommodation Lesotho offers a variety of accommodation options including hotels, lodges, guest houses, mountain resorts, and camping sites. Visitors can enjoy both luxury stays and affordable lodging.
Transport Public taxis are commonly used in towns, while rental cars are recommended for exploring mountain areas. 4x4 vehicles are best for rough roads and remote villages.
Safety Travel in groups when possible, especially in remote areas. Keep valuables secure and follow local advice when hiking or exploring unfamiliar places.
Best Time to Visit April to October is ideal for cool weather, mountain hiking, and sightseeing. Winter months may also bring snow in the highlands, creating beautiful scenery.
Currency The official currency is the Lesotho Loti (LSL), which is equal in value to the South African Rand (ZAR). Both currencies are widely accepted.
Language Sesotho and English are the official languages of Lesotho. English is commonly spoken in tourist areas, hotels, and businesses.
Food Visitors can enjoy traditional Basotho meals such as papa, moroho, and grilled meat. Many restaurants also offer international cuisine and local specialties.
Weather Lesotho experiences cool temperatures throughout the year due to its high altitude. Warm clothing is recommended, especially during winter and evenings in the mountains.
Tourist Attractions Popular attractions include Thaba Bosiu, Maletsunyane Falls, Katse Dam, Sehlabathebe National Park, and the Maluti Mountains.
Activities Visitors can enjoy pony trekking, hiking, mountain biking, fishing, cultural tours, skiing, and photography in Lesotho’s scenic landscapes.
